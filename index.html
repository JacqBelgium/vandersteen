About me

Over 50 years in IT — still building.

My career in IT started in 1972, as an employee. A few years later I took the step into entrepreneurship and built companies — from startup to mid-sized business. I'm not a developer by trade, but I spent years leading teams of developers, which taught me exactly what a good application needs: not just technically sound, but genuinely useful and easy to use.

Today I put that experience into practice myself. With Claude AI as my development partner, I build handy, well-thought-out apps — without writing every line of code myself, but with the insight of half a century in IT to make the right choices.
